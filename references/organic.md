# Organic short-form video analysis reference

Load **after `shared.md`** only when `brief.md` declares `Intent: organic`. Do not use this file to infer that a video is organic.

Organic analysis asks whether the piece gives viewers reasons to keep watching, send, save, replay, discover the topic, and understand why this creator/account is worth another view. Use the account's own comparable posts as the performance baseline; do not import universal ranking weights or benchmark percentages.

## Organic judgment axes after the shared three

The first three qualitative axes are defined in `shared.md`:

1. Frame 0 + Hook
2. Retention
3. Payoff + Proof

Then add organic-specific judgments:

4. **Send / Save / Replay value** — is there a concrete social, future-use or rewatch reason?
5. **Search + topic clarity** — can a new viewer understand the topic and phrase it clearly enough to seek/recognise it again?
6. **Originality + creator ownership** — could this only come from this creator/account, or could anyone post it unchanged?

Use short qualitative judgments with evidence. **Do not calculate a cross-axis average.** Any blocker from `shared.md` stays above the evaluation rather than being diluted by stronger axes. Report execution risk separately.

## Sends, saves and replays — ask behavior questions, not weights

Never write “shares count X times more,” “saves have Y% weight,” or a completion threshold as if the platform published those numbers.

Ask instead:

- **Send:** is there a specific person this viewer would think of, and what makes sending it useful/funny/identity-relevant?
- **Save:** does the video contain a checklist, reference, recipe, steps, template, comparison or future-use detail worth returning to?
- **Replay:** is there a missed detail, satisfying transformation, fast reveal, dense demonstration, visual loop or first/last-frame connection worth seeing again?
- **Comment:** is there a genuine decision, experience, disagreement or open question — not empty engagement bait?

If the piece does none of these, say that the ending has no organic after-action rather than inventing an algorithm penalty.

## Ending action and loop design

Choose one primary ending behavior. Do not staple “like/comment/follow” onto every video.

### Send ending

Best when the idea has a recognisable person/use case: “this is you,” a warning, shortcut, identity joke, or a problem two people share. The send reason should exist even without an explicit prompt.

### Save ending

Best when the payoff remains useful later: steps, settings, checklist, reference, template, places, ingredients, prompts, decision criteria.

### Replay / loop ending

Use when the last beat can naturally reconnect to the first frame/line, reveal an easily missed detail, or make the transformation satisfying to watch again. A loop must preserve meaning; do not cut off a conclusion merely to manufacture a replay.

### Comment ending

Use for a real choice, specific question, disagreement, experience report or critique invitation. Do not withhold a necessary answer merely to farm comments.

## Search clarity

Search-oriented clarity is a writing discipline, not a hidden-keyword trick.

When natural, align one clear topic phrase across:

- spoken line early in the video
- readable on-screen text
- caption/title

Write for humans first. Reject keyword stuffing, invisible text, fake locations, Hidden Words tricks and claims that metadata hacks guarantee reach.

For Xiaohongshu, the **cover + title are a hook layer before frame 0** because the discovery surface is a two-column card feed. Review cover/title separately from the opened video's first frame.

## Non-follower reach

Treat non-follower reach as an outcome to diagnose, not a ranking formula.

Ask:

- can a stranger understand the topic without prior account context?
- does frame 0 identify the problem/result/subject quickly enough?
- does the payoff stand alone without inside knowledge?
- is the creator's distinctive angle visible, or only obvious to followers?
- is there a send/save/replay mechanism that works outside the existing audience?

Compare against the creator's own comparable posts when data is available.

## Trial Reels

Use Trial Reels as an experiment surface, not a loophole. Meta's official description says Trial Reels are shown to non-followers first and can later be shared more broadly.

Workflow:

1. Test one core idea with a meaningful change in frame 0, hook, structure, proof, pacing or payoff.
2. Move one major variable at a time so the result teaches something.
3. Compare trial variants with prior trials / comparable content, not a universal benchmark.
4. Promote/remake the clearest learning rather than repeatedly uploading identical copies.

Official source, read 2026-08-17:
- https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/

## Breakout mechanisms — borrow the mechanism, not the surface format

A breakout post is evidence that an acquisition event happened, not proof that the exact format is repeatable. Check the second and third uses before calling a format repeatable.

Useful mechanisms from the retired organic strategist:

### 1. Unnamed useful artefact

Show a real tool/system/template visibly doing useful work. Curiosity comes from wanting the artefact or understanding the setup. Do not manufacture a tease around something trivial.

### 2. Visible constraint

State a small number of constraints the viewer can verify in-frame and that make the task genuinely harder. The constraint must fit the creator's real positioning.

### 3. Invited critique / live self-score

A visible count, mistake tally or self-scoring device can make viewers inspect the performance. It only works when the counted thing is real and relevant.

### 4. Single take under a visible clock

A real uninterrupted performance plus a visible clock can prove difficulty. It is invalid when the “live” result was visibly pre-made; never fake time pressure over prepared assets.

### 5. Identity share vs debate

Choose the social job. A piece designed so sharing says something about the viewer is structurally different from a piece designed around an unresolved argument. Do not force both into one ending.

For every breakout mechanism, ask what part is transferable and what part depended on novelty, timing, existing audience, personality or one-off context.

## Organic performance diagnosis

Use the account's actual Insights when available:

- early retention / retention chart
- average watch time and completion relative to the video's own length
- replays
- sends/shares
- saves
- non-follower reach
- profile visits/follows when relevant to the goal

Meta officially exposes Replays and retention-chart style insight surfaces for Reels; that supports analysing those behaviors without claiming a numerical ranking weight.

Official source, read 2026-08-17:
- https://about.fb.com/news/2023/11/new-ways-to-create-content-on-instagram/

Interpretation questions:

| Pattern | Question |
|---|---|
| weak early retention | is frame 0 unclear, slow or mis-signalling the topic? |
| strong opening then early drop | did the hook overpromise or did the body switch into explanation? |
| watchable but low saves/sends | is there any future-use or specific-person value? |
| high replay behavior | is it a satisfying loop/detail or confusion forcing a rewatch? |
| follower reach strong, non-follower weak | does the video depend on account context a stranger lacks? |
| reach strong, profile action weak | does the post connect back to a clear creator/account position? |

Views alone do not establish a repeatable winner.

## TikTok and Douyin are independently operated products for analysis

Do not transfer a TikTok behavior claim, product feature or assumed ranking rule onto Douyin, or vice versa. ByteDance lists Douyin and TikTok as separate products serving different markets; treat their product behavior independently unless each platform's own evidence supports the same conclusion.

Official source, read 2026-08-17:
- https://www.bytedance.com/en/products

### Douyin saves/favorites

Douyin's privacy policy says personalized recommendation may use behavior records including search, click, posting, follows, favorites/saves, browsing, downloads and shares. It does **not** publish numerical weights for those behaviors.

Therefore ask: **does this piece give the viewer a reason to save/favorite it?** Never assign a save-weight percentage.

Official source, read 2026-08-17:
- https://www.douyin.com/privacy/

## Organic output

Follow the report structure in `SKILL.md`. Within it, include the organic-specific judgments and the strongest next edit/test when relevant. Do not add an overall numeric score.

When generating concepts rather than reviewing an existing video, name the mechanism, the exact frame-0 action, the promise, the payoff and the intended ending behavior. Do not promise virality.
